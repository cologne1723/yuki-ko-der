## 문제

문장 `Hello! You're new here, right? It's nice to meet you.`를 `N`줄 출력하세요.

## 입력

`1\le N\le10`인 정수 `N`이 주어집니다.

## 출력

지정된 문장을 `N`줄 출력하세요.

## 예제

### 예제 1 {file=""}

#### 입력

```text
1
```

#### 출력

```text
Hello! You're new here, right? It's nice to meet you.
```

#### 입력

```text
2
```

```text
Hello! You're new here, right? It's nice to meet you.
Hello! You're new here, right? It's nice to meet you.
```
